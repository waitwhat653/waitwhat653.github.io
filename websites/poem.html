Beauty Art

A Villanelle Variation by Deoteet Ojasavee

Art is beauty within all
Seeing life in anything
Pieces of paper, stone, sound,
film, dance, words, pictures,
It can be literally anything.

Paint splishing and splashing and getting messy
Colors getting mixed, new things are born
Warm and cool and bright and dark,
True hues, Fade shades, Lone tones,
Art is beauty within all.

Notes, as Wholes or Halves
Rests or Sound
Long or Short
Then chorus, giving the life to music
It can be literally anything.

You see, art is subjective,
Yet objective still.
Art is beauty within all
It can be literally anything.
