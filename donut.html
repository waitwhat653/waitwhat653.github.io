A donut is a type of deep fried dough confectionery or dessert food. The donut is popular in many countries and prepared in various forms as a sweet snack that can be homemade or purchased in bakeries, supermarkets, food stalls, and franchised specialty outlets.

Did you know? Donut can also be spelled "doughnut."

The two most common types are the toroidal ring donut and the filled donut—which is injected with fruit preserves, cream, custard, or other sweet fillings. A small spherical piece of dough may be cooked as a donut hole. Other shapes include rings, balls, and flattened spheres, as well as ear shapes, twists and other forms. Donut varieties are also divided into cake and risen type donuts.
